# Team Photo Instructions

## How to add your real team photo:

1. Save your Code Origin.ai team photo as `team-photo.jpg`
2. Upload it to this folder (`public/images/`)
3. The website will automatically display your real team photo!

### Image Requirements:
- Filename: `team-photo.jpg`
- Recommended size: 1200x900 pixels or larger
- Format: JPG
- The photo you shared with the red/coral polo shirts is perfect!

### Where the photo appears:
1. Hero section (landing page)
2. About section

Once uploaded, your real team photo will replace the placeholder image.
